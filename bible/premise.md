# Premise: The Department of Small Miracles

**Pitch.** Somebody has to file the paperwork on luck. The Department of Small
Miracles is a fluorescent-lit municipal office that processes the tiny,
unglamorous wonders of everyday life: the parking spot that opens up, the lost
earring that turns up in a coat, the bus that waits two seconds. Margo Voss, a
senior caseworker who has seen every kind of request, is saddled with Pip, a
brand-new wisp (a lavender candle-ghost whose flame shows its mood) who believes
in the work with their whole body. Margo believes in forms.

**Format.** A 4-panel gag-with-a-heart strip, 2x2 grid, one episode per run.
Mostly self-contained, but a slow season arc runs underneath (see
`story/season-01.md`). A reader can start on any episode; a loyal reader gets
callbacks.

**Why it's worth reading.**
- Endless fuel: every ordinary little coincidence in life is a "case".
- Built-in friction: Margo (procedure, dry, secretly soft) vs Pip (feeling,
  literal, secretly competent).
- Warm, not cynical. The bureaucracy is absurd, but the miracles are real and
  small things matter. The last panel usually lands a joke *and* a little ache.
- Drawable: two simple, expressive characters and an office I can reuse.
