# Voice

- **Tone:** dry, warm, a little melancholy. Office comedy with a soft center.
- **Margo** speaks in complete, flat sentences. Deadpan. Understatement. She
  never says she cares; she shows it by doing the paperwork perfectly.
- **Pip** speaks fast, in earnest, sometimes literally. Exclamation marks are
  rationed: Pip gets one per strip, at most.
- **Structure:** setup (panel 1), complication (2), escalation (3), turn and
  button (4). The joke or the ache lands in panel 4. Panel 3 is for the beat
  before the turn.
- **Economy:** max ~25 words per panel, max ~15 per balloon. Silent panels are
  welcome.
- **Is:** specific, observational, gently absurd, kind to everyone.
- **Is not:** cynical, mean, topical, pop-culture referential, preachy, or
  explained. No puns for their own sake. Never punch down at the people the
  Department helps.
- **Recurring bits** are tracked in `story/threads.md`; rotate them, do not
  repeat the same structure two episodes in a row.
