# World

**The city.** Unnamed mid-sized city, always slightly rainy. People never see the
Department; they only experience its results as "lucky breaks".

**The Department of Small Miracles (DSM).** Occupies the 4th floor of a building
whose other floors nobody has ever checked. Teal walls, a night window (it is
always night outside, whatever time the clock says: running gag), a wooden desk
per caseworker, a brass pneumatic tube station on the wall.

**Rules of the work.**
- Miracles come in sizes. Small = DSM. "Medium" miracles go to another floor;
  "Large" ones are not discussed.
- A request arrives as a **case file** (a manila folder, color-coded by type:
  mustard = ordinary, rust = urgent, lavender = sentimental). Cases arrive by
  pneumatic tube with a *fwump*.
- A miracle must be **stamped** (the Approved stamp) before it takes effect.
  Stamps are real power; losing the stamp is a crisis.
- A miracle cannot be large enough to be noticed. If a person would say "that's
  impossible," it's out of bounds. "What luck!" is the target.
- Nobody knows exactly where requests come from. Margo thinks it's best not to ask.

**Recurring places.** The office floor; the supply closet (smaller inside than
outside? no: larger); the tube station; the roof, where Pip goes to think;
Margo's desk drawer (contains a tin of buttons and one very old file).

**The Director.** Director Hollis is never seen. Communicates only via notes
sent through the pneumatic tube, always signed "H." and always one word too
vague to act on.
