# 0017 – Light Mist
Logline: Fen, a raincloud from Medium Miracles, drips into the office with a "super small" request and is far too proud to admit they've locked themselves out of the fifth floor.
Beat: Season 2 beat 5 (Fen comes downstairs: drizzling, lost, needs a Small favour stamped, too proud to say so). Pays off the rain under the door (0016). Fen drives it; Margo does not deflect with paperwork (0016 structure note): she gets one dry line. Pip leads the case. Callback: anonymous requests (0013).
Layout: 4 panels, 2x2
