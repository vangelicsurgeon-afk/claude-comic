# 0011 – Found by Requester
Logline: A very old lavender case comes back by tube, as it does every year, and the requester turns out to be Margo, age seven.
Beat: Season 1 beat 11 (a lavender case Margo has seen before, decades ago; the old file). Pays off the pearly top button (0008), the pink copies "for someone who might come looking" (0003), the very old file in the drawer, and Margo's "That's not a category." Retro-0010 asked for room: 5 panels, two rows of two plus one wide panel.
Layout: 5 panels; 2 + 2 + 1 wide (1000 x 1280)

## Panel 1
Staging: Office, night window. Pip at the tube station (left) holding a faded lavender folder at arm's length, flame small and wary. Margo at her desk (right), eyebrows up, looking toward the tube.
Dialogue:
- PIP: Lavender. Case 0212. The paper's gone soft.
- MARGO: Again.
Caption/SFX: FWUMP (small, near the tube)

## Panel 2
Staging: Margo's desk, drawer open. Margo lifts out the very old file: a fat bundle of identical lavender folders tied with string. Pip beside the desk reads the label of the new one, worried.
Dialogue:
- PIP: Requester: M. Voss, age seven.
Caption/SFX: (none)

## Panel 3
Staging: Close-up of Pip reading the request slip aloud, then looking up (eyes up) at Margo's chest.
Dialogue:
- PIP: "Find Mum's pearl button. Please."
Caption/SFX: (none)

## Panel 4
Staging: Close-up of Margo, one hand at her mismatched pearly top button (large, pearl-white against the mustard), eyes down at Pip, softened (worried brows, not deadpan). Pip small at the lower left.
Dialogue:
- MARGO: I came looking. I found it myself.
- PIP: Then stamp it.

## Panel 5 (wide)
Staging: Wide. Margo behind her desk (left half) holding the stamp poised above a big form on the desk. On the form, a new hand-drawn box, lettered FOUND BY REQUESTER, with a tick in it. Pip (right) holding a pen, proud. The tied bundle of old lavender folders on the desk. Night window behind.
Dialogue:
- MARGO: "Found by requester" isn't a category.
- PIP: It is now.
Caption/SFX: (none; the stamp is about to come down)
