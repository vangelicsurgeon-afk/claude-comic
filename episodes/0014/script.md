# 0014 – Box Four
Logline: A reprinted batch of case forms turns up in the supply closet, and FOUND BY REQUESTER, the box Pip drew by hand in 0011, is now printed on them.
Beat: Season 2 beat 2 (FOUND BY REQUESTER on official forms; Margo says nothing and keeps one blank). Leaves the desks for the supply closet (first on-page visit; world rule: it's larger inside). Callbacks: 0011 "It is now.", 0009 the stamp "filed" in Margo's bun. Plain funny, with a crumb of ache.
Layout: 4 panels, 2x2

## Panel 1
Staging: Wide. Office wall, no desks. The supply closet door stands open; through the doorway, shelves recede far back, much deeper than the wall could allow (larger inside, never remarked on). Pip floats out of the doorway hugging a fat new ream of forms tied with a paper band, flame tall. Margo stands beside the door with her mug, deadpan, looking at the ream.
Dialogue:
- PIP: The new forms came.
- MARGO: They reprint them every forty years. To move a comma.

## Panel 2
Staging: Medium two-shot. Pip has torn the band and holds one sheet up close to their face, reading; eyes wide, flame starting to rise. Margo sips from her mug, half-lidded, not looking.
Dialogue:
- PIP: They didn't move a comma.

## Panel 3
Staging: Silent insert. Close-up of the printed form. Teal header "FORM 1 · CASE CLOSURE". Three printed tick boxes, same typeface: STAMPED / EXCEEDS SMALL (REFER UP) / FOUND BY REQUESTER. Small print at the bottom: "Rev. 11/26 · Approved: H."
Dialogue: (none)

## Panel 4
Staging: Two-shot. Pip faces Margo, holding the form up high in both hands, flame huge, mouth open. Margo stands deadpan, looking away toward the closet. Folded neatly and tucked into her bun, sticking out like the stamp in 0009, is one blank form. She says nothing.
Dialogue:
- PIP: Margo, they printed my box!
