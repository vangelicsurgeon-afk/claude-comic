# 0016 – Intent to Knock
Logline: Margo climbs one flight of stairs only to prove there's nothing to see, finds a door marked 5, and explains with a fan of forms why nobody knocks; then it turns out to be raining under the door.
Beat: Season 2 beat 4 (the stairwell; a door marked 5; Pip wants to knock; Margo explains, at length and with forms, why nobody does). Pays off 0015's open "Please come up further. -H." (she goes up exactly one flight, then stops). Plants Fen (beat 5): the drizzle under the door. Leaves the desks; includes a close-up, per the 0015 notes. No hidden-care button.
Layout: 4 panels, 2x2

## Panel 1
Staging: NEW SETTING: the stairwell. Concrete-grey walls, a flight of stairs rising left to right, a metal handrail, a landing at the top right with a plain door, a big painted "5" on the wall beside it. Pip has floated ahead up to the landing, flame tall, pointing at the 5. Margo is on the lower steps, climbing, holding the "further" note, a stack of forms tucked under her other arm, deadpan.
Dialogue:
- MARGO: One flight. Only to show you there's nothing to see.
- PIP: There's a door.

## Panel 2
Staging: The landing. Pip at the door, small fist raised to knock, flame tall. Margo has arrived on the landing behind Pip, one hand raised ("stop"), the stack of forms under her other arm. Pip looks back over their shoulder.
Dialogue:
- MARGO: Nobody knocks. Form 12. Notice of Intent to Knock.

## Panel 3
Staging: CLOSE-UP of Margo, cropped at the chest, half-lidded, holding up a fan of four forms like a hand of cards; each has a visible big number on its header: 12, 12-B, 12-C, 12-D. Grey stairwell wall behind.
Dialogue:
- MARGO: 12-B, the knock. 12-C, being let in. 12-D, being let out again.

## Panel 4
Staging: Wide again on the landing. Pip has sunk down to the bottom of the door: a small grey puddle seeps out from under it, with three falling drops near the gap. Pip points at it, flame leaning (puzzled). Margo is already one step down the stairs, turned away, forms tucked under her arm, deadpan.
Dialogue:
- PIP: Margo. It's raining under the door.
- MARGO: Then it's a Medium problem.
