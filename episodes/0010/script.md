# 0010 – Two Minutes
Logline: Pip takes Margo up to the roof to show her that it is morning somewhere, and Margo rations herself a sunrise.
Beat: Season 1 beat 10 (the roof; real sky). Pays off the always-night window. Breaks the paperwork-deflection run (0008, 0009): the setting changes and Margo does not file anything.
Layout: 4 panels, 2x2

## Panel 1
Staging: Office, night window. Pip tugs Margo's cardigan sleeve toward a small door at the left marked ROOF. Margo stands, holding a folder, unmoved. Pip's flame tall.
Dialogue:
- PIP: Come up. Just for a minute.
- MARGO: It's night.

## Panel 2
Staging: Wide shot, the roof door (left) open on a pale sky. Rooftop with a water tank, vent pipes, low parapet. Pip already out, waving. Margo stepping out through the door, one hand lifted to shade her glasses, wary.
Dialogue:
- PIP: Not up here.

## Panel 3
Staging: Silent. Wide. The sun rising behind the city skyline, peach and gold sky. Margo and Pip small from behind at the parapet, side by side. Margo's cardigan top button catches the light.
Dialogue: (none)

## Panel 4
Staging: Close-up on the two of them, Margo in profile facing the sun, face lit, softened (happy), Pip looking up at her.
Dialogue:
- MARGO: Two minutes.
- PIP: One fifty-nine.
