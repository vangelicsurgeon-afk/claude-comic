# 0012 – Not Slower
Logline: The Hollis note that sat unread in the tube since 0009 turns out to be Pip's probation review, and Margo writes it in one line.
Beat: Season 1 beat 12 (probation review, Margo's one-line evaluation). Resolves the unread Hollis note (0009). Pays off Margo's dry compliments, from 0001's "Good. Now do it again, slower."
Layout: 4 panels, 2x2

## Panel 1
Staging: Office, night window. Pip at the tube station (left), peering into the open hatch, where a small folded note has sat for a week. Margo at her desk (right), mug, looking over at Pip. In 0009 she ordered "Do not read it."
Dialogue:
- PIP: Your note from last week is still in the tube.
- MARGO: Read it.

## Panel 2
Staging: Pip holds the note open, flame leaning (flustered). Margo at her desk, already taking a white form out of the open drawer, deadpan.
Dialogue:
- PIP: "Please review. -H." Review what?
- MARGO: You.

## Panel 3
Staging: Silent insert. Close-up of the form on the desk, Margo's hand and pen. Printed: PROBATIONARY REVIEW / Name: PIP / Evaluation (one line): and, in Margo's handwriting, "Good. Now do it again."
Dialogue: (none)

## Panel 4
Staging: Two-shot. Pip holding the review form up in both hands, flame tall (proud), looking up at Margo. Margo behind her desk sips from her mug, half-lidded, looking at the night window, not at Pip.
Dialogue:
- PIP: No "slower"?
- MARGO: Not anymore.
