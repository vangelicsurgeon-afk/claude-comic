# 0018 – Fourteen Hundred and Forty
Logline: Case 4790, refused as "Exceeds Small" back in 0007, comes back by tube; this time Pip doesn't go out, and Margo, who knows about cases that keep coming back, starts counting.
Beat: Season 2 beat 6 (Case 4790 comes back by tube, as unclosed cases do: the 0011 rule). Quieter and Pip-led, with Margo back in focus (0017 note).
