# Season 2: "Upstairs"
Pip is a full caseworker now. The building widens: the 5th floor, Medium Miracles,
the place refused cases go and nobody downstairs visits. Arc: Pip gets curious
about upstairs; Margo has spent forty years not going there. The season asks
whether a Medium miracle is just a lot of Small ones done carefully. Ends with
Margo climbing one flight of stairs.

## Looking back at Season 1
- **Worked:** kindness delivered as paperwork (0007, 0008, 0011, 0012); held
  silences (0006, 0010); callbacks that pay off a single word (0010 "One fifty-nine",
  0012 "slower"); leaving the office now and then (0006, 0010); one big
  layout for the emotional centre (0011).
- **Stale:** desk-bound two-shots; Margo deflecting by documentation; Pip asks and
  Margo deflects. Season 2 needs more places, a third voice on-page, and some
  episodes that are plain funny with no ache at all.
- **Evolve:** add one new drawn character (Fen, below); let Pip lead more cases;
  make the 5th-floor visits the new "roof" (sparingly, it is a treat).

## New character (to design at beat 5)
**Fen**: a Medium Miracles caseworker from the 5th floor. A small grey raincloud in
a neat tie, floating; drizzles when stressed, which is often. Speaks in
initiatives and acronyms; thinks Small is adorable. Secretly envies how much
Margo's floor actually notices people. They. Build `art/characters/fen.py` plus
a model sheet before first use.

## Beats (guide, not script; mark [x] when done)
1. [ ] Pip's own desk: Margo gives them a desk (small, next to hers, as far from the tube as hers is) and their own stamp. Pip's first solo case.
2. [ ] FOUND BY REQUESTER appears printed on a fresh batch of official forms. Somebody upstairs adopted Pip's category. Margo says nothing; she keeps one blank.
3. [ ] A Hollis note: "Please come up. -H." Up where? Margo stamps it "handled" without going. Pip notices she didn't.
4. [ ] The stairwell. A door marked 5. Pip wants to knock; Margo explains, at length and with forms, why nobody does.
5. [ ] Fen comes downstairs (first on-page visitor): drizzling, lost, needs a Small favour stamped and is too proud to say so.
6. [ ] Case 4790 (refused "Exceeds Small" in 0007) comes back by tube, as unclosed cases do (0011 rule).
7. [ ] Pip goes upstairs alone. The 5th floor: brighter, emptier, its window shows late afternoon (the floors have different times; never explained).
8. [ ] Margo's idea: split Case 4790 into many Small miracles, each one stampable. Enormous paperwork. Pip loves it.
9. [ ] The split case, done: one quiet panel of the requester's ordinary lucky day, made of tiny luck.
10. [ ] Fen and Margo: a Form exchange (Medium needs Small's sign-off for once). Respect, in triplicate.
11. [ ] Pip fills in a Form 9 (Request for Recess) for Margo, pink copy kept by Pip.
12. [ ] Margo climbs one flight of stairs. We see the door open, not what's behind it.

Running items to rotate in: the tube and FWUMP, the button tin, the roof at sunrise
(once at most), Margo's mug, Hollis notes ("Please ___. -H."), Pip's one exclamation
mark, Pip now counting durations like Margo.
