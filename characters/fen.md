# Fen
Medium Miracles caseworker, 5th floor. A small grey raincloud in a neat teal tie, floating about a hand's
height off the floor. They.

- **Wants:** to be taken seriously; for the 5th floor's numbers to look good. Underneath: to be noticed
  the way Margo's floor notices people. Envies Small without ever saying so.
- **Voice:** brisk and corporate. Initiatives, acronyms, "circle back", "going forward", "at scale". Calls
  the 4th floor's work "adorable" and means it as a compliment. Never asks for help directly: frames every
  favour as a "cross-floor synergy opportunity". Short sentences when rattled.
- **Tells:** drizzles when stressed (the drizzle level is their mood gauge, like Pip's flame; never mentioned
  in dialogue). Straightens their tie when lying or embarrassed (pose `tie`). Leaves small puddles.
- **Sample lines:** "Quick ask. Super small. Medium-small." / "We don't do 'what luck' upstairs. We do 'outcomes.'"
  / "This is fine. This is a light mist." / "Is that a button tin? That's so Small."
- **Relationships:** Margo (wary respect; Fen thinks she is a legend and would never say so); Pip (Fen finds
  them adorable; Pip finds Fen fascinating and asks about the rain); Hollis (Fen has "never met him either").
- **Rules:** Fen comes from the 5th floor, where the window shows late afternoon (beat 7). The rain under the
  5th-floor door (0016) is Fen's. Never explain how a cloud fills in forms.
- **Kit:** `art/characters/fen.py` (`draw(pose, expression, facing, look, drizzle)`). Model sheet
  `art/model-sheets/fen.png`. Built 2026-11-23, before first appearance.
