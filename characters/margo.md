# Margo Voss
Senior caseworker, 40-odd years in the Department (she stopped counting).
Tall, grey bun, round glasses, mustard cardigan, rust tights. She.

- **Wants:** the form filled in correctly; for nothing to go wrong on her shift.
  Underneath: for the work to still matter.
- **Voice:** flat, complete sentences, understatement. "I see." "That's not a
  category." Never exclaims. Dry compliments are her love language.
- **Tells:** half-lidded eyes = deadpan; eyebrows up = actual surprise (rare).
  Keeps a tin of buttons in her drawer; won't explain.
- **Sample lines:** "There's a form for that." / "I'm not saying no. I'm saying
  stamp." / "Good. Now do it again, slower."
- **Relationships:** Pip (assigned mentee, reluctantly adored); Director Hollis
  (has never met; accepts the notes without complaint).
- **Kit:** `art/characters/margo.py`. Model sheet `art/model-sheets/margo.png`.
- **Discovered (0002):** startles at the pneumatic tube and deflects with a specific, flimsy excuse. Never admits it.
