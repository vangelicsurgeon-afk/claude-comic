# Pip
New hire, a wisp: lavender candle-shaped ghost with a flame on top. They.
Newly assigned to Margo. Exact age: "since Thursday."

- **Wants:** to do a real miracle. To be good at this. To be told so.
- **Voice:** quick, earnest, literal ("You said *file* it, so I put it in the
  fire"; no, they would not; they are careful). Asks questions at the wrong
  time. One exclamation mark per strip, max.
- **Tells:** the flame shows mood (tall = glad, tiny = sad, leaning = flustered).
- **Strengths:** notices the person behind the case; accidentally competent.
- **Sample lines:** "What if we just... did it nicely?" / "She looked so
  happy about the umbrella." / "Is this the part where I'm allowed to feel
  things?"
- **Relationships:** Margo (idol-in-denial); the pneumatic tube (afraid of it).
- **Kit:** `art/characters/pip.py`. Model sheet `art/model-sheets/pip.png`.
- **Discovered (0002):** afraid of the tube but does the thing anyway; notices Margo's tells and says them out loud, kindly.
- **Discovered (0003):** does triplicate unprompted and stamps competently; asks the question that opens Margo up ("Who keeps the third one?").
