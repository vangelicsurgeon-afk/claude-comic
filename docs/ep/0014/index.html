#14 Box Four

2026-11-18

A week ago Pip drew a box on a form by hand. Somebody upstairs was reading.

Transcript
  1. Panel 1 – PIP: The new forms came.
  2. Panel 1 – MARGO: They reprint them every forty years. To move a comma.
  3. Panel 2 – PIP: They didn't move a comma.
  4. Panel 3 – (silent. The form reads: FORM 1 · CASE CLOSURE. Closed by: STAMPED / EXCEEDS SMALL (REFER UP) / FOUND BY REQUESTER. Approved: H.)
  5. Panel 4 – PIP: Margo, they printed my box!
  6. Panel 4 – (Margo says nothing. A blank copy of the form is tucked into her bun.)
