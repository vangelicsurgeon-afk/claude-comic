#15 Come Up

2026-11-20

Margo has been asked to go upstairs for forty years. She has found a great many ways to comply without going.

Transcript
  1. Panel 1 – SFX: FWUMP
  2. Panel 1 – PIP: "Please come up. -H." Up where?
  3. Panel 1 – MARGO: He doesn't say.
  4. Panel 2 – MARGO (standing on her desk): Up.
  5. Panel 3 – SFX: KA-CHUNK (the note is stamped HANDLED)
  6. Panel 3 – PIP: I think he meant the fifth floor.
  7. Panel 3 – MARGO: Then he'd have said "floor."
  8. Panel 4 – SFX: FWUMP
  9. Panel 4 – NOTE: Please come up further. -H.
  10. Panel 4 – PIP: He says "further."
